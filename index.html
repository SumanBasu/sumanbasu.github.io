01

The person in the room

Charm, to me, is not a costume. It is paying attention - to a sentence, a screen, a case that only fails on Thursdays. I work from Kolkata, on systems people actually depend on. I like jokes that arrive a half-beat late, and failures that arrive for a reason.

One side of me wants to take a product apart until the mechanism is obvious. The other wants to leave a little mystery in the room. If you picked light or dark at the door, you already met that split.

02

What I bring

Habits that travel. They work on software. They also work on rooms full of people.

  • Social engineering

    The legal kind. Reading a room, finding the real objection, getting a sceptical person to move without making a scene. People are a system. I pay attention to that one too - which is most of management, whether or not anyone prints the title yet.

  • Taste

    Type, colour, silence. I care how a thing feels before it explains itself - a product, a deck, a sentence. I will argue for one good typeface over five mediocre ones, and for a release that does not look like it was in a hurry.

  • Quality, as a people problem

    Tests matter. Mentoring matters more. I would rather teach a team to catch the crack than be the only person who can. The work is half automation, half getting a date to become honest.

  • A voice

    Words with a temperature. I would rather a site - or a report, or a standup - sound like someone than like a template that escaped a conference booth.
